Building Winosaurus meant keeping up with what the wine world was actually talking about. Decanter, Wine Enthusiast, Wine Business, Wine Berserkers, WineAmerica. I was opening all of them anyway.

The Dashbordeaux scrapes those feeds, writes a daily briefing, and keeps an archive you can step through by date. There's a second view for the industry numbers: economic impact by state, market metrics, the stuff that usually lives in a PDF.

My goal is to become a dashboard for those who are also interested in wine, viticulture, and enology. Data is building and visuals and stats are incoming.
