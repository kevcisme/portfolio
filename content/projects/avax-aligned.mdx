I wrote this site for a friend. It's basically a threaded style comment section but it's meant for people who trade meme coins to post anonymously. 
Other fun feature is a "meme gallery" where all these degens can post their memes about coins.

Also, because design is my passion, I wrote it with this "glass effect" that Apple was obsessed with (at the time I coded it out).

Was a lot of fun deploying this one because I got to work through CA certificate stuff. Deployed it with a Makefile. Working slowly on getting better at developing CI/CD pipelines!
