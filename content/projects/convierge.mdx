Every time I travel I end up Googling the same stuff. Do I tip here? What's 22°C in real clothes? How do I say thank you? What's the emergency number?

Convierge is the app I wanted in my pocket for that. Currency and tipping rules, unit conversions, phrases, country and city guides, and emergency info, organized by country and available offline.

There's also a dream board and itinerary. Paste an Instagram or TikTok link and it pulls the places out of the post, so a camera roll full of screenshots can actually turn into a trip.
